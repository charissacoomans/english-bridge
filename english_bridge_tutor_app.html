Suggested 45–60 minute first lesson

  1. 5 min — introductions and learning goals.
  2. 20–25 min — placement test (allow the student to work independently).
  3. 10 min — short speaking conversation to check fluency and confidence.
  4. 10–15 min — review answers and teach one useful point.
  5. 5 min — agree on a learning goal for next time.

Teacher note: This is an informal diagnostic, not an official CEFR certification. Use the score alongside speaking, listening and writing observations before assigning a level.
