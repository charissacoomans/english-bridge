📚 English Tutoring – Student Guidelines / 英語課程須知

Clear expectations help us make every lesson useful and enjoyable.

Welcome! I’m excited to help you achieve your English goals. Please review the guidelines below.

歡迎！很高興能協助你提升英語能力。請先閱讀以下課程須知：

💳 Rates & Packages / 課程費用與優惠

  • Single Session (單堂課): NT$ 900 / 1.5 hours (1.5小時)
  • 5-Session Package (五堂優惠包): NT$ 4,000 (Save NT$ 500! / 省下500元)
    Note: Packages are paid in full in advance and non-refundable.
    (須全額預付，購買後不可退費)
  • Payment Method (付款方式): Cash only / 僅限現金 (TWD)

🎁 Loyalty Card / 集點卡獎勵

  • Bring your card to every lesson to get 1 sticker per session.
    每上完一堂課即可獲得 1 個貼紙（請記得隨身攜帶集點卡）。
  • Collect 5 stickers to unlock a FREE 30-minute bonus session on your 6th appointment!
    集滿 5 個貼紙，第 6 次上課即可獲得 免費額外 30 分鐘加課！

🌟 Intake & Materials / 初次體驗與教材

  • Free Intake (免費諮詢 30 分鐘): 30-minute chat to test your level and discuss goals.
    包含 30 分鐘程度測試與學習目標討論。
  • Learning Materials (學習教材): You are welcome to bring your own English books.
    學員可自行攜帶想學的英文教材或書籍。

⏰ Cancellation Policy / 請假與調課規定

  • 24-Hour Notice (24小時前通知): Free rescheduling if cancelled at least 24 hours in advance.
    上課前 24 小時以上通知，可免費更改時間。
  • Late Cancellation (臨時取消): Cancellations under 24 hours will incur a 50% fee at the next session.
    24 小時內臨時取消，需於下次上課補繳 50% 課程費用。
  • Punctuality (準時): Lessons end at the scheduled time even if you arrive late.
    請準時出席；若遲到，課程仍會按原定時間結束。

📍 Location & Expenses / 上課地點與費用

  • Venue (地點): Public cafes or libraries in Taoyuan City.
    桃園市區內的公共咖啡廳或圖書館。
  • Expenses (餐飲費用): Drinks/food purchased at the venue are at your own expense.
    低消餐飲費用由學員自行負擔。
Thank you for reading! I look forward to learning with you. / 感謝閱讀，期待與你一起學習！
